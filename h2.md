# Favourite color: purple
x) Pyramid of pain:<br>
-Pyramid of pain shows how different indicators affect an attacker based<br>
on how hard they are to change. The higher you go in the pyramid,<br>
the harder it is for the attacker to continue their operations.<br>
<br>
Diamond model:<br>
-Diamond model in a nutshell: "attacker(1.) uses certain technique(2.)<br> 
via infrastructure(3.) against the victim/target(4.)"<br>
1. Attacker = adversary<br>
2. Technique = capability<br>
3. Infrastructure = infrastructure<br>
4. Victim = victim/target<br>
<br>
Imagine a situation where you receive an email that seems to be from your<br>
ISP. The email contains a link. If you click the link, you may be taken<br>
to a malicious website that attemps to trick you into entering sensitive<br>
information or downloading malware onto your device.<br>
<br>
1. Adversary is the attacker/group of attackers doing phishing<br>
2. Capability is the phishing-email <br>
3. Infrastructure is the server or IP address used in the attack<br>
4. Victim is you<br>
